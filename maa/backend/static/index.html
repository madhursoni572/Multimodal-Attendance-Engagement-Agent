How it works

From raw footage to decisions in four steps.

1

Detect and track

Faces are found and followed as anonymous IDs (P1, P2…).

2

Listen

Noise level and speech-versus-silence are measured per window.

3

Fuse and score

Rule-based reasoning combines both signals into a High, Medium or Low engagement level.

4

Advise

The agent flags drops, silent stretches, late arrivals and suggests what to try.

Privacy by design

Participants are anonymous IDs and no faces are stored or matched to identities. Uploaded video is deleted as soon as analysis finishes. Everything runs on your own server, and the optional cloud language model sees only aggregate numbers, and only if you add a key.
